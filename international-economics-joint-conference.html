International Economics Joint Conference (IEJC)

I hope to continue organizing an annual conference on international economics in China’s Greater Bay Area—a dynamic region comprising China's Guangdong Province, Hong Kong SAR, and Macau SAR. In addition to its vibrant economy and central role in global trade and technology, the region offers a favorable climate, rich natural scenery, and growing investment in research and education. My goal is to bring together resources from multiple universities across the region to establish the first annual academic conference series on international economics in this area.
